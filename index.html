Kuldevi & Kuldevta Ritual Calendar

Three yearly occasions to keep our Kuldevi (Amba Maa) and Kuldevta (Bapa) pleased, as recorded in the family booklet:

1. Chaitra Sud Dasam

Lapsi-Khichda offering (Nived). Falls in the Gujarati month of Chaitra (roughly March–April).

2. Aaso Sud Dasam

Lapsi-Khichda offering (Nived). Falls in the Gujarati month of Aaso (roughly September–October).

3. Aaso Vad Chaudas (Kali Chaudas)

Rice offering (Gotrij). Falls just before Diwali, in the dark half of Aaso.

These are lunar-calendar dates, so the exact Gregorian date shifts every year. Search "[occasion name] + this year" online closer to the date, or check a Gujarati Panchang, for the precise date.
